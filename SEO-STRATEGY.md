# SEO strategy

**Market evidenced by the current page:** Auckland Region, New Zealand. The site describes a mobile mechanic visiting customers' homes or workplaces. Do not target other cities/suburbs until actual service coverage is confirmed.

## Audience and intent

- Auckland drivers seeking a mechanic who can come to home or work.
- High-intent needs expressed on the current page: vehicle servicing and repairs, diagnostic reports, engine/transmission work, electrical repairs/accessories, oil/fluid service, brakes/suspension/WoF report repairs, and hybrid/high-voltage vehicle service.
- The main conversion actions are phone and email. The service dialog opens those actions and does not constitute an online booking system.

## Keyword-to-page map

There is one public route; fragments are navigation anchors, not independent SEO landing pages. This avoids cannibalising a single page into near-duplicate suburb pages.

| Existing route/section | Primary intent | Supporting intent from visible services |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` home | mobile mechanic Auckland; mobile mechanic near me (when locally relevant) | vehicle servicing Auckland; car repairs Auckland; vehicle diagnostics; engine/transmission repairs; auto electrical repairs; oil and fluid service; brake and suspension repairs; WoF repairs; hybrid vehicle servicing |
| `/#services` | Service discovery within homepage | Diagnostics & reports; engine & transmission; electrical & accessories; oil & fluid; brakes, suspension & WoF repairs; hybrid & high-voltage vehicles |
| `/#about` | Mobile service model and Auckland-area relevance | mechanic at home or workplace; mobile vehicle service |
| `/#contact` | Contact and directions intent | AutoMate Lanka phone, email, address and published weekday hours |

Do not add claims for pre-purchase inspection, fleet work, emergency/24-hour service, brand specialisation, EV battery repair, same-day work, certifications, or pricing without business confirmation and visible evidence.

## Local search and Google Business Profile

- Keep the name/phone/service-area identity consistent across site and profiles. Confirm whether a street address is suitable for public display and whether this is a service-area business with no customer-facing premises.
- Owner should verify primary and secondary Google Business Profile categories against currently available NZ categories and actual services; likely primary category to evaluate is “Mechanic”/“Auto repair shop” (choose the available category that best describes the actual business). Do not select hybrid/EV specialist categories unless capability is confirmed.
- Verify phone, website, hours, service area, services, appointment link and holiday hours. Use real business/work photos only with owner consent.
- Request honest feedback from completed customers through a neutral, direct follow-up; do not offer incentives, filter who can leave a review or write reviews for customers. Reply accurately and protect customer/vehicle details.
- Build consistent, legitimate listings in relevant NZ/Auckland directories and automotive associations only where eligible; correct stale records rather than creating duplicates.
- Maps placement depends on relevance, distance and prominence and cannot be guaranteed by on-page SEO.

## Competitor snapshot

Search results sampled on 26 November 2026 surfaced mobile-service competitors including [Mobile Mech](https://www.mobilemech.co.nz/), [Vehicle Doctorz](https://vehicledoctorz.co.nz/), [Mobile Autoworks NZ](https://www.mobileautoworksnz.com/app/), [Osmoz Auto](https://www.osmozauto.co.nz/) and [Garage on the Go](https://www.gogarage.co.nz/). The visible differentiators used by these sites include detailed service pages, explicit service coverage, brand/credential claims, pricing/booking tools and project imagery. These observations are qualitative search-result/site observations only, not ranking or traffic metrics.

Opportunity for AutoMate Lanka: make the accurate mobile service model and actual service coverage unambiguous, keep phone/email easy to crawl and use, maintain a complete Business Profile, and gather genuine work evidence and reviews. Do not copy competitors' claims or add unverified differentiators.

## Authority, AI discovery and conversion

- Keep business identity and services consistent between visible HTML, JSON-LD, directories and the Business Profile.
- Make the canonical homepage, sitemap and contact actions reachable without JavaScript; core page content is static HTML.
- Do not create hidden AI text, speculative AI metadata, or generic generated repair advice. Crawling is open to ordinary public search crawlers via `robots.txt`; no special model-training policy existed to preserve.
- Track completed contact outcomes only if a privacy-compliant analytics setup is approved. No analytics ID, conversion endpoint, or online booking is present in this repo.
