# 30 / 60 / 90-day organic growth plan

This plan assumes the business and service facts on the current site are accurate. The current hosting alias is confirmed; address privacy, operating hours and exact service footprint still need owner review.

## Days 1–30: establish clean signals

- Verify HTTPS, canonical, sitemap and robots responses on the current `automatelanka.koungasolutions.co.nz` host.
- Verify the Google Business Profile owner, name, category, address/service-area mode, phone, website, hours, services and appointment path. Correct duplicates and inconsistencies.
- Verify the domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap and inspect the canonical homepage.
- Check the phone/email actions on mobile and desktop and record real completed customer enquiries manually until suitable conversion tracking is approved.
- Ask recent customers neutrally for an honest review after service; respond accurately and protect personal information.
- Record starting Search Console impressions, queries, clicks and calls/enquiries; separate branded from non-branded demand.

The move to the proper domain is a later migration task. Before that move, choose the destination, reconcile existing public-domain content, set one-hop redirects, then update and verify canonical, structured data, social URLs, sitemap, robots, Search Console and Bing properties together.

## Days 31–60: improve based on real query data

- Review index coverage, chosen Google canonical, queries and device/location performance weekly.
- Compare impressions and engagement for mobile mechanic Auckland, vehicle servicing, repairs, diagnostics and only those service terms confirmed by the business.
- Correct listing data at legitimate local directories; prioritize accurate existing citations over volume.
- Obtain owner-approved original photos and factual experience/qualification details if available; consider visible trust additions only after approval.
- Review contact conversion friction and mobile performance with real user/device data. Make design changes only through a separate approval.

## Days 61–90: build evidence-backed authority

- Decide whether service-specific pages are justified by confirmed demand, distinct expertise and sufficient original information. Avoid thin suburb doorway pages.
- Publish only owner-reviewed automotive guidance or case studies based on real work, with technical review and customer/vehicle privacy protection.
- Review authentic customer feedback and improve service descriptions based on actual customer questions.
- Compare Search Console clicks and completed calls/enquiries against the initial period; report direction and sample size without promising rank gains.
- Recheck domain consistency, structured data, sitemap, broken links, indexing and mobile page experience; update the SEO checker when architecture changes.

## Ongoing measures

- Search Console: clicks, impressions, query mix, indexed canonical, page experience.
- Business Profile: discovery searches, calls, directions, website actions, review response coverage (only when account owner provides access).
- Business outcomes: completed enquiries/bookings, source where known, service category; do not count a button click as a completed booking.
- Performance: field Core Web Vitals when sufficient real-user data exists; treat Lighthouse as a lab diagnostic, not a guarantee.
